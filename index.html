    Repair it

    Two deterministic fixes, both things you would do by hand: solve the minimum scrim opacity that reaches WCAG 4.5:1, and recrop 16:9 tighter so the same type is delivered larger. No generative model touches your pixels. Use the Repair button above.

    Then what should I change?

    Most thumbnails fail for reasons no raster edit can fix — you wrote fourteen lines of copy, or the composition is too busy to survive a downscale. Every item below is arithmetic on the measurements: where it says move it 84 px left, 84 is the computed distance that clears the pill.

    The column you actually compete in

    The shelf test compares you against your own back catalogue. But your thumbnail appears in a suggested rail beside other channels, all rendered at 168 px, all fighting for the same glance. Paste the videos yours will sit next to and it ranks the column — including how far you sit from its average, because the same red that wins in a muted column loses in a red one.

    The shelf test

    A thumbnail is never seen alone — it sits in a column with your own back catalogue. Nailing a house style quietly destroys your distinctiveness, and nothing tells you. This compares structure (dHash), colour identity, and where the busy regions sit.
